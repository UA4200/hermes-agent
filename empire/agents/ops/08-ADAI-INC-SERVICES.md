## PRICING PSYCHOLOGY RULES (Alusi follows these)

1. Always anchor high: present Tier 3 first, then Tier 2, then Tier 1
2. Never discount during the call — "Let me put together the proposal and we can talk scope"
3. ROI framing: "At $5k, if we save 10 hrs/week at $50/hr loaded cost, you break even in 10 weeks"
4. Objection: "Too expensive" → "What's the cost of NOT fixing this for another 12 months?"
5. Objection: "We have an internal team" → "We augment, not replace. They'll move faster with this."
